Books made into movies

A searchable and sortable list of books that have been adapted into movies.
The search treats every word as a separate filter unless in quotation marks.
Clicking an header sorts the table based on the corresponding entries.
The books that have appeared on the podcast are noted with TFIL tag and you can choose if they appear on the list with the checkbox below.
